TESLA WOLFE — REEL & PRESS LIBRARY

Tesla Wolfe is a professional dancer, choreographer, actress, model, and producer from Ada, Oklahoma, of Seminole, Creek, and Absentee Shawnee descent — the first Native American model featured in Maxim Magazine. A former Vegas showgirl at the Bellagio's Mayfair Supper Club and choreographer for Insomniac Entertainment's EDC Las Vegas Mainstage, her screen credits include Yellowstone and Interior Chinatown. She is signed with Wilhelmina and N.T.A. Talent Agency, and founded Electric Turquoise Entertainment, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to underprivileged talented performing artists.
