Tesla Wolfe is a professional dancer, choreographer, actress, model, producer, and entrepreneur from Ada, Oklahoma, of Seminole, Creek, and Absentee Shawnee descent — the first Native American model ever featured in Maxim Magazine. A former Vegas showgirl at the Bellagio's Mayfair Supper Club and choreographer for Insomniac Entertainment's EDC Las Vegas Mainstage, her screen credits include Yellowstone and Interior Chinatown. She is signed with Wilhelmina Modeling Agency, NTA Talent Agency and The Option Agency.

She trained at the Lee Strasberg Film & Theatre Institute and the Ivana Chubbuck School of Acting, and attended the University of Central Oklahoma as a Dance major with a minor in Psychology. Her movement background spans ballet, jazz, contemporary, hip-hop, and pole, sharpened over years as a company member with 8&1 Professional Jazz & Contemporary Company.

She travels internationally as a runway model in Native American fashion and was honored as the first LA Chapter Council Member of the International Indigenous Fashion Council. A sought-after speaker, she presented her workshop "The Electric Effect" as a keynote presenter for the Native Action Network's 21st Annual Native Women's Leadership Forum and Young Native Women's Leadership Academy, and as an instructor at the UNITY Mid Year Youth Conference 2026.

Her full acting, dance and runway credits, reels, and choreography follow below. Tesla is also the founder of Electric Turquoise Entertainment, a Native-led performing arts nonprofit.
